3. SELLER BALANCE MANAGEMENT & WITHDRAWALS
Quy dinh ve thanh toan va thu nhap danh cho Nguoi ban tren Shopee:
- So du Tai khoan Shopee (Shopee Balance): So tien tu cac don hang hoan tat se duoc ghi co
vao So du Tai khoan Shopee cua Nguoi ban sau khi tru cac khoan phi dich vu va phi giao dich.
- Rut tien tu dong (Auto Withdrawal): Nguoi ban co the thiet lap lich rut tien tu dong hang
tuan (vao thu 3) hoac hang thang (ngay 1 va 15) ve tai khoan ngan hang chinh thuc.
- Rut tien thu cong (Manual Withdrawal): Nguoi ban co the gui yeu cau rut tien bat ky luc nao.
Thoi gian xu ly chuyen tien tu 24h den 48h lam viec (khong tinh thu 7, chu nhat va ngay le).
- Han muc rut tien: Moi tai khoan Nguoi ban duoc mien phi 01 lan rut tien thu cong moi ngay.

4. TRANSACTION FEES AND PLATFORM COMMISSIONS
Nguoi ban va Nguoi mua phai tuan thu quy dinh ve cac khoan phi giao dich va phi nen tang:
Transaction Fee
(bao gom tien hang va phi van chuyen). Muc phi hien tai la 3.0% - 4.0% tuy theo phuong thuc.
Fixed Fee
cua Nguoi ban Shopee Mall va Nguoi ban thong thuong (tu 2.5% den 8.0% tuy danh muc nganh hang).
Service Fee
Goi Freeship Xtra, Goi Voucher Xtra, hoac Chuong trinh Hoan Xu Xtra.

5. TRANSACTION SECURITY AND ANTI-FRAUD MEASURES
Doi voi an toan thanh toan va phong chong gian luan:
- Shopee su dung he thong quan ly rui ro tu dong de phat hien cac giao dich bat thuong,
su dung the tin dung gia, hoac co hanh vi spaming ma giam gia.
- Moi hanh vi co tinh thuc hien giao dich khong hop le, rua tien, hoac gian luan thanh toan
se bi khoa tai khoan vinh vien va chuyen ho so sang co quan chuc nang xu ly theo phap luat.
- Shopee khong bao gio yeu cau Nguoi dung cung cap ma OTP, mat khau vi hoac so the tin dung
qua dien thoai hay tin nhan ca nhan.
