1. OVERVIEW OF SELLER LISTING REGULATIONS
Van ban nay quy dinh cac tieu chuan, dieu kien va quy dinh bat buoc danh cho Nguoi ban
khi dang tai, quan ly va kinh doanh san pham tren san thuong mai dien tu Shopee Vietnam.
Tat ca Nguoi ban phai tuan thu Luat Thuong mai dien tu, Luat Bao ve quyen loi nguoi tieu dung
va cac quy dinh phap luat hien hanh cua Nuoc Cong hoa Xa hoi Chu nghia Viet Nam.
Viec dang ban san pham vi pham se dan den cac hinh thuc xu phat tu xoa san pham, cong diem
sao qua ta den khoa tai khoan kinh doanh.

3. PRODUCT TITLE, IMAGES AND DESCRIPTION STANDARDS
Quy dinh chi tiet ve thong tin dang tai san pham:
- Tieu de san pham (Product Title): Phai bat dau bang Ten san pham + Thuong hieu + Thong so.
Tieu de phai viet bang tieng Viet co dau hoac tieng Anh. Khong chua tu khoa rac, khong gia danh.
- Hinh anh va Video san pham: Hinh anh phai sac net, phan anh chinh xac san pham thuc te.
Khong duoc dung hinh anh phan cam, khong chua thong tin/logo cua cac san TMDT doi thu.
- Mo ta san pham: Cung cap thong tin chi tiet ve chat lieu, kich thuoc, xuat xu, huong dan.

4. PRICE SETTING AND INVENTORY MANAGEMENT RULES
Nguoi ban phai tuan thu cac quy dinh ve gia va quan ly ton kho:
- Niem yet gia minh bach: Gia san pham phai niem yet bang Dong Viet Nam (VND), bao gom
tat ca cac khoan thue ap dung. Nghiem cam dang gia 0 dong, gia ao hoac khac xa gia tri.
- Hanh vi tang gia roi giam gia (Spam Discount): Nghiem cam hanh vi tang gia san pham len cao
ngay truoc khi tham gia chuong trinh khuyen mai roi thuc hien giam gia ao.
- Cap nhat ton kho chinh xac: Nguoi ban phai thuong xuyen cap nhat so luong ton kho thuc te.
